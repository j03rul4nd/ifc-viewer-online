Shopify bulk import builder

Add 3D models to many products at once. Files stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.

  1. In Shopify, go to Products → Export and download the CSV.
  2. Pick that CSV and your model list below.
  3. Download the result and import it with Products → Import, ticking "Overwrite products with matching handles".
