The problem

Bad models are found by whoever receives them

Under ISO 19650-2, reviewing and accepting the information model is an activity of the appointing party: the contractor or developer who receives it. The UK BIM Framework guidance describes that check as part of the common data environment workflow.

Skanska UK says its goals depend "critically" on getting quality information from its supply chain. Contractor BIM managers describe considerable difficulties getting information down and back up the chain by email and FTP.

In practice a coordinator opens each delivery, finds the same missing properties and wrong names, writes the reasons by hand and waits for the next version. The subcontractor only learns the rules after being rejected.

Sources: UK BIM Framework, Guidance Part C (read on 2026-11-11), solibri.com, Skanska UK case (read on 2026-10-28), Construction Management (read on 2026-10-28)

How it works

From your requirements to the client report

Every capture below is the Reception design with sample data (project "Mirador del Turia", ten suppliers). The interface is in Spanish.

  1. Preview

    Publish your requirements as a link

    Rules, IDS and minimum score by ISO 19650 status. Subcontractors get the approved version; an older link keeps working.

  2. Preview

    Subcontractors check for free before sending

    No account. They see the score, what fails, the elements involved and the fix guide for each rule, so most errors are fixed before they reach you.

  3. Preview

    Review a queue, not an inbox

    Deliveries arrive already checked, with the file name read against ISO 19650 and a before-and-after against the previous version. Keyboard shortcuts take you through the queue.

  4. Preview

    Return it with reasons the supplier can act on

    Each reason points to the elements in 3D and to how to fix them. Everything is logged with a chained hash and exported to CSV.

  5. Preview

    See clashes between disciplines over time

    Clash runs in the browser, ordered by estimated cost, with the trend between runs.

  6. Preview

    Keep the client informed without writing it

    A project page with the latest score, accepted deliveries, resolved clashes and the next milestone. Sending is simulated in the preview.

  7. Preview

    Send the weekly report to site management

    Received, passed, missing and the estimated time saved, written for you to copy or download.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Can I use Reception today?

Not yet. It is designed and shown with sample data. If you receive models from subcontractors, talk to us: we are looking for a few pilot projects. Checking a model against your requirements is free and works today in the viewer.

Do subcontractors pay or need an account?

No. They open your link and check their model for free in the browser. Paying is for whoever receives and manages the deliveries. How the upload page works: an upload page for suppliers without an account.

Does it replace Catenda, Dalux or Autodesk Construction Cloud?

No. It checks the model before it reaches your CDE and works whichever CDE you use. Each CDE has its own workflow; see the comparison above with the date we read their pages.

Is the model uploaded to check it?

For the free check, no: it runs in the subcontractor's browser. In Reception, the delivery you accept is stored for you in the EU.

How much will it cost?

It is planned from €290 per project a month with 15 suppliers, or €790 for unlimited projects. Prices may change before launch; see the plans.

Where does the savings figure come from?

From the assumptions in the table above: number of deliveries, minutes per task and the share each task saves. They are assumptions, not measurements, and you can change them in the overview.
