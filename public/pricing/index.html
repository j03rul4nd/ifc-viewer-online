Questions

Which plan is right for me?

If you open and check models, Free. If you send models to clients on your own, Pro. If several people in a firm deliver models, Team, from three editors. If you collect models from subcontractors, Reception.

Will the free viewer stay free?

Yes. Everything that runs in your browser stays free for good: viewer, measuring, sections, Health Score, 44 rules, IDS 1.0, BCF and the SDK. Paid plans only add what needs our servers, or new tools for teams.

Can I pay today?

Not yet. Paid plans open to the waitlist first. Leave your email and we write once, when your plan opens. Prices may still change before launch; the waitlist keeps the founding offer.

Where are files stored on paid plans?

On servers in the EU. On the free plan nothing is uploaded: your browser opens the file.

Do my suppliers pay for Reception?

No. Suppliers check their files against your requirements free, in their browser. Reception is paid by whoever receives the deliveries.
