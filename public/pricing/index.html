Questions

Will the free viewer stay free?

Yes. Everything that runs in your browser stays free for good: viewer, measuring, sections, Health Score, 44 rules, IDS 1.0, BCF and the SDK. Paid plans only add things that need our servers, or new tools for teams.

Can I pay today?

Not yet. Paid plans open to the waitlist first. Leave your email and we write once, when your plan opens. Prices may still change before launch; the waitlist gets the founding offer.

Where are files stored on paid plans?

On servers in the EU. On the free plan nothing is uploaded: your browser opens the file.

Do my suppliers need to pay for Reception?

No. Suppliers check their files against your requirements free, in their browser. Reception is paid by whoever receives the deliveries.
