IFC EMBED

Embed an IFC model anywhere

Paste a public IFC URL, tune the look, and copy the iframe (or link). Works in blogs, CDE panels, Notion, Power BI and dashboards — the model is parsed in the visitor’s browser, nothing is uploaded.

Must be reachable over HTTPS with CORS enabled.


        
💡Where can I paste this? Anywhere that accepts an iframe or a web URL: blog/CMS embeds, a CDE document panel, Notion, Confluence, a Power BI “Web content” visual, SharePoint, or any dashboard.
