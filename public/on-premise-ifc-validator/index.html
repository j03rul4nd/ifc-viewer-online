FAQ

Questions


Does anything leave our network?

No. The deployment has no outbound connection and sends no telemetry. Models, results and logs stay on your hardware. Updates are delivered as signed packages through the channel you choose (USB, internal registry).

What runs on it?

The same engine as the public app: the 3D IFC viewer, the 44-rule validator with the Health Score, full IDS 1.0 checks, BCF export, multi-model coordination, measurement and section tools — served from a container on your own server or gateway.

Can we use our own hardware?

Yes. We can deliver the container image for your own server or virtual machine, or a preconfigured industrial gateway. For defence and critical-infrastructure buyers we offer EU-sourced hardware.

Is the public app enough instead?

For many teams, yes: the public app already processes IFC files only in the browser. The on-premise option is for organisations whose policy forbids loading software from the internet at all, or that need an audited, version-pinned deployment with support.

How does a pilot work?

A fixed-scope pilot on one site: install, configuration with your IDS/EIR requirements, a training session for your BIM team and a support window. You decide afterwards whether to roll it out.
