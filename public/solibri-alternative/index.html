Free Solibri alternative for checking an IFC before it is delivered

Solibri is a rule-based checking platform sold in plans from €99 to €2,772 per licence per year. IFC Viewer Online is a free web page that opens an IFC, runs 44 fixed rules and your IDS, and shows what fails. It replaces Solibri for some jobs and not for others; this page says which.

Choose Solibri when you coordinate several disciplines and need real clash detection, when your organisation writes its own rules, or when checking has to be governed across many projects. Nothing on this page does that.

Choose IFC Viewer Online when you want to know today whether one IFC is fit to send: identifiers, structure, names, properties, your IDS. It is also the tool for the people around a Solibri team who have no licence: the architect exporting the model, the client receiving it.

Many teams will use both: a quick check in the browser before export, and Solibri for coordination.

Solibri today

If you are looking for Solibri Office or Solibri Anywhere

Solibri's site lists Solibri Office, CheckPoint, Anywhere and Site as legacy products that are "no longer available for new customers". The current plans are Starter, Essential, Advanced, Premium and Security+.

The free version of Solibri Anywhere still runs for people who already have it, on versions before 26.4.0; Solibri says the free entitlement is not available in newer releases. If you used Anywhere to open and look at IFC files, the viewer does that for free, in the browser, on any operating system.

Scope

What you get here, and what you do not

You get

  • A Health Score from 0 to 100 and the fixes that recover the most points.
  • The failing elements highlighted in the 3D model, and listed by rule, storey or class.
  • IDS 1.0 checks and five built-in validation profiles, from basic delivery to ISO 19650 and LOD 300.
  • Automatic fixes for GlobalIds and names, manual edits of names and property values, and the corrected IFC.
  • Findings as BCF 2.1, CSV, JSON, an HTML report or a COBie spreadsheet.

You do not get

  • Clash detection between models, or at the level of the real geometry.
  • A rule editor, code checking (accessibility, fire, escape routes) or space analysis.
  • Issue tracking with assignments on a server. BCF files are exchanged by hand.
  • Files of 2 GB or more. The limit depends on the memory of your computer.

FAQ

Questions

Is IFC Viewer Online a replacement for Solibri?

For checking one IFC before delivery (identifiers, spatial structure, names, properties, IDS), yes. For clash detection between disciplines, custom rules and governance across projects, no: that is what Solibri Essential and Advanced are for.

How much does Solibri cost?

Solibri publishes starting prices per licence per year: Starter €99, Essential €1,428, Advanced €2,109 and Premium €2,772. Security+ is priced on request (solibri.com/pricing, read on 2026-11-10).

Is there a free version of Solibri?

Solibri Anywhere, which had a free version, is now a legacy product and is not available to new customers; existing free users can keep versions before 26.4.0 (solibri.com/products/legacy-products, read on 2026-11-10). The cheapest current plan is Starter at €99 a year.

Does the €99 Starter plan check models?

According to Solibri, Starter is for visual inspection and for reviewing results made by others: "Model checking is performed in Solibri Essential, Advanced, or Premium" (solibri.com/products/starter, read on 2026-11-10).

What does Solibri do that this cannot?

Clash detection on real geometry and between models, rules written for your organisation, quantity takeoff rules, native Revit models, BCF servers and CDE connections, and a fully offline plan. See the table above.

Can I use both?

Yes, and it is a common split: authors and clients check a file in the browser before it is sent, and the coordination team runs Solibri on the federated model.
