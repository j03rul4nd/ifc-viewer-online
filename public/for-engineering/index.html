The problem

Rejections come from details nobody checked

Clients now send their own information requirements, often as IDS. A delivery that misses a property name or a classification goes back, and the fix costs more than the check would have.

One BIM consultant describes an IFC data validation problem that cost weeks, caused by property names that did not match what was required. ISO 19650-2 makes checking before sharing a step of the delivery team, not of the client.

In a studio the rules live in one person's head or spreadsheet, clashes are reviewed in a meeting, and the issues end up in email threads without the view that explains them.

Sources: BIM Corner (read on 2026-11-11), UK BIM Framework, Guidance Part C (read on 2026-11-11)

How it works

From company rules to a published model

Every capture below is the Team design with sample data. The interface is in Spanish.

  1. Preview

    Write the rules once, as sentences

    Class, condition, requirement and severity, including geometry (clearance in front of doors, free height) and relations (door in a wall). Every rule is IDS 1.0.

  2. Preview

    Govern them as a company

    A shared library with versions, author, review and approval (the author does not approve their own rule), comments and export to IDS.

  3. Preview

    Run clashes in the browser

    Hard and clearance clashes between disciplines, grouped, with rules to ignore what is accepted and an estimated cost for each.

  4. Preview

    Turn every failure into an issue with a fix

    Assigned, with a deadline, the saved camera and a suggested fix per authoring tool from our remediation guides. Guests comment for free; BCF in and out.

  5. Preview

    Stop a weak model before it is published

    Nothing reaches Published below your minimum score, with an optional reviewer and a history of what passed and why.

  6. Preview

    Keep the client informed

    Updates by phase built from live data: score, deliveries, issues and milestones. Sending is simulated in the preview.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Can I use Team today?

Not yet. It is designed and shown with sample data. Join the waitlist to hear when it opens. Checking a model with your IDS is free and works today in the viewer.

Is clash detection free?

No. Clash detection between disciplines is a new tool and it will be paid. Opening, federating and checking each model stays free. More in the post on clash detection in the browser.

Can we use our client's IDS?

Yes, and that part is free today. In Team you can also import an IDS into the company library and keep its versions.

Does it replace BIMcollab or Solibri?

Not for every team. See the comparison above, with the prices from their pages and the date we read them.

How much will it cost?

It is planned at €59 per editor a month billed yearly, three editors minimum; guests are free. Prices may change before launch; see the plans.

Can we check a whole folder of models?

That is part of the paid preview too: read how batch validation works.
