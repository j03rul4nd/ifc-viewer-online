Why it stays out of your page’s way
About 30 KB, in two steps
A 3 KB loader reserves the space, so nothing jumps. The 27 KB engine arrives when the map nears the screen. No map library, no tile server.
Nothing to consent to
No cookies, no analytics, no calls to third parties. The world map ships inside the file, so no visitor’s IP goes to a map provider.
Yours to restyle
Colours, font, logo and language in the form. Your own CSS can set --tg-accent and friends and reach parts with ::part().


Questions
Is it a real-time GPS tracker?
No. It draws the data you give it: dates, progress or positions from your own system. It does not connect to carriers or vessels. Routes are schematic lanes, not navigation.
Can I show simulated shipments?
Yes, for demos and sales pages. They always carry a visible “Simulated data” label, so nobody takes them for real freight.
How do I keep shipment data private?
Do not paste it into the snippet. Point the widget at your own endpoint and use the lookup, which fetches one reference at a time under your own rules.
Does it work in SharePoint?
Through the Embed web part, as an iframe, once a site owner allows the domain. Scripts in page content do not run there. We have not tested it in a real tenant.
Does it work on Taobao?
Not as a live widget, as far as we can confirm. You can publish an image of the map and host the live version on your own page.
Does it cost anything?
No account and no fee. It runs in the visitor’s browser, so there is no server of ours to pay for.
Is the email kit free?
No. The widget for your website is free; the email kit and tracking page builder are part of the Pro plan, which is not on sale yet.
Can the live 3D map run inside an email?
No. Email clients do not run scripts. The email carries an image or GIF and a personal link; the interactive map opens on the tracking page.
How do I measure sales from it?
Your email platform counts clicks on the link. Every link out of the page carries campaign tags, so your shop analytics can attribute sales. Compare with a group that receives the plain email to measure the real lift.
