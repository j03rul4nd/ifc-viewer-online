Questions

Who is this for?

Companies that buy from factories in China through a sourcing agent and want one view of every order instead of chats, spreadsheets and forwarder emails.

Is the data in the demo real?

No. Orders, vessels and suppliers are simulated and marked as such. Routes and ports are real.

Does it calculate duties?

The demo estimates duty from an HS code and a rate per order. A real setup uses your own tariff data and broker figures.
